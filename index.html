About us

From the island to Cobb Parkway.

In the Dominican Republic, the barbershop is where the neighborhood comes together — for the cut, the conversation, the bachata in the background and the dominoes after. Angel Dominican Barbershop brought that tradition to Kennesaw.

  • Serving Kennesaw from 2790 Cobb Pkwy NW
  • Hablamos español — and English
  • Walk-ins welcome seven days a week
  • Community pop-ups and local vendor days

    FAQ
    Do I need an appointment?

    No — walk-ins are always welcome. If you want a specific barber at a specific time, send a booking request above.

    Do you cut kids' hair?

    Absolutely. Our barbers are patient with little ones, and first haircuts are a specialty.

    How do I pay?

    There's an ATM right inside the shop. Ask your barber about card and app payments.

    ¿Hablan español?

    ¡Claro que sí! Our team speaks Spanish and English.

    Is the shop accessible?

    Yes — there's wheelchair-accessible parking and a restroom on site.
